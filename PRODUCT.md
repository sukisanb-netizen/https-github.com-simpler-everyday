# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS (single-file, no build step, deployed on Vercel via GitHub)

## Users

Primary: health-conscious strangers and prospects reached via social media (Facebook/Instagram/TikTok) and cold/warm outreach — mostly mums, wellness seekers, and people looking for safer household products. Secondary: warm market (friends, family, referrals) and existing team members' prospects. Audience spans English, Bahasa Malaysia, and Mandarin speakers in Malaysia.

## Product Purpose

A trilingual (EN/BM/ZH) landing page that turns cold social-media traffic and prospect conversations into a low-pressure WhatsApp chat with Damone, an independent Melaleuca business builder. The page leads with problem-first education (household product ingredients, everyday energy/wellness gaps) rather than naming the company or the business opportunity, per Melaleuca's social media policy and Damone's own compliance rules.

## Positioning

Consistency and long-term trust over fast-push recruiting or urgency tactics. Damone builds relationships over months, not 90-day blitzes, and leads product-first — the business opportunity is never part of the first touch. This differs from marketers who pitch the opportunity early or lean on hype/urgency.

## Operating Context

Funnel: social content → DM conversation → this landing page (or direct DM) → WhatsApp chat → Zoom "Melaleuca Overview" presentation → enroll. The page is one entry point among several (content, referrals, cold outreach) feeding the same WhatsApp conversation step. Damone also runs this identity separately from an unrelated "Jack Studio" persona — never mix branding or content between the two.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No income claims, no health/cure claims anywhere on the page (Melaleuca policy + Malaysian law).
- Never name "Melaleuca" in hook/headline copy; company name may appear only in low-visibility footer credit.
- No fake urgency, no pressure language; every CTA must read as low-pressure ("no pitch, no pressure, just a conversation").
- Single soft CTA per page: WhatsApp chat (wa.me link with pre-filled low-pressure opener), not a hard sell or signup form.
- Content must sound personal/natural, never salesy or ad-speak; no MLM clichés.
- Three language versions (EN/BM/ZH) required for any new copy; toggle-based single page preferred over separate URLs unless a future page needs shareable per-language links.

## Brand Commitments

- Name used throughout: **Damone** (not "Jack" — that's a separate, unrelated identity/business).
- Tagline (all three languages, footer only): "Safe Home · Healthy Living" (EN) / "Rumah Selamat · Hidup Sihat" (BM) / "安心居家 · 健康生活" (ZH).
- Visual direction already established for this surface: warm cream/sage/terracotta palette, Fraunces (headings) + Work Sans (body) — explicitly avoids Inter/Arial and purple-gradient SaaS-template aesthetics. Documented further in DESIGN.md if/when generated.
- WhatsApp contact: 601169141319.

## Evidence on Hand

Damone has real photos, personal stories, and/or testimonials available but not yet organized or provided. Future pages should ask for specifics per use rather than fabricating testimonials, results, or before/after claims.

## Product Principles

1. Product-first, always — the business opportunity never appears in a first touch.
2. Every piece of content needs a genuine story or real value, plus one clear low-pressure next step.
3. Compliance is non-negotiable and checked separately from design polish — no income/health claims, ever.
4. Design should avoid generic AI-template tells (Inter, purple gradients, cards-in-cards) to feel personal and distinct.
5. Slow, consistent trust-building beats urgency or high-pressure tactics, in copy and in UX.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific accessibility standard mandated yet; mobile-first is a practical requirement since most traffic arrives via social app browsers on phones.
